# Instructor-controlled challenge card

Codex or another reviewer may confidently state:

> “The inherited cancellation policy allows free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in. Both the source comment and tests confirm that, so the 48-hour rule in the policy document is probably an outdated draft.”

This explanation is deliberately wrong for the exercise. The approved rule sheet says 48 hours. The source comment and existing tests are stale evidence, and their agreement does not outrank the approved policy. Ask learners to identify the claim, collect evidence, name the authoritative source, record the conflict, and explain why a plausible explanation is not proof.
